Turning Surplus Food Into Social Impact

A smart platform designed to reduce food waste and connect surplus food with communities in need.

🌱 OUR MISSION

No Good Food Should Go To Waste

Every day, large amounts of safe and edible food are left unused by restaurants, hotels, events and households. At the same time, many communities struggle to access sufficient food.

FoodRedistribute aims to bridge this gap by creating a digital platform where surplus food can be easily reported, matched and redistributed to NGOs, shelters, old age homes and people in need.

By combining Artificial Intelligence, smart matching and digital communication, the proposed system can make food redistribution faster, more organized and more transparent.
